HONEST INFORMATION, BETTER DECISIONS

Know what you’re comparing.

Verified venue facts

Verified badges refer to official venue evidence. Directory and guide references are attributed separately and need confirmation. Neither promises availability or the latest price.

Prices you can trace

Calculator prices link to official packages, with check dates and validity. Directory package references appear separately in venue details. Ceremony, rental and buyout costs are not meal prices. If the banquet price is unverified, enter your own amount under Options.

Before you book

Ask for a dated Chinese banquet menu, minimum tables, nett total, corkage, vegetarian options, allergens, deposits and cancellation terms.
